# Q1 demo video script (target 1 minute 50 seconds, audience: senior executives)

Record the Dialogflow CX simulator with a screen recorder, then upload to Stream or Echo360.

| Time | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:15 | Flow graph | Staff report leaks, wasted energy and access barriers in many ways, and reports often arrive late or incomplete. This assistant captures them in under a minute and sends them to the right team. It is built in Dialogflow CX. |
| 0:15 to 0:50 | Type "There is a water leak in Building C that has been running all morning." then "banana pancakes" then "it can wait, nobody is at risk" | One sentence fills in the issue and the building, so the bot asks only for urgency. A nonsense reply gets a gentle reprompt. A routine answer ends with a confirmation, and the report is structured data that an ESG dashboard can use. |
| 0:50 to 1:15 | Reset the simulator. Type the water leak message, then "immediate, water is flooding the corridor" | An urgent answer takes a different branch. The bot tells the user to call 000 and connects them to a person, because safety should not wait for a form. |
| 1:15 to 1:35 | Reset. Type "what data do you collect about me" | The bot explains what it collects and asks users not to share personal details. Privacy and oversight are part of the design. |
| 1:35 to 1:50 | Type "purple elephants", "asdf qwerty", "zzz blah", then show q1_dialogflow/workflow_concept.png | If the bot cannot understand after two tries, it hands over to a person. The next step is a pilot at one site, with the bot connected to ticketing and to the ESG dashboard. |
